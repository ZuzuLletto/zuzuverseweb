In-App Purchases, Subscriptions & Premium Features

Zudoku provides optional Premium purchase options and auto-renewable subscriptions managed through the in-app purchase infrastructure (IAPManager). Payment and transaction processing are handled directly by official app store platforms (Apple App Store / Google Play Store).

  • Monthly Subscription (Monthly Premium):
    • Product ID: zudoku_monthly_premium
    • Type: Auto-Renewable Subscription
    • Features & Benefits: Removes all mandatory interstitial and banner advertisements and unlocks all game themes.
    • Billing & Cancellation: Renews automatically on a monthly basis. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via your device's App Store / Google Play account subscription settings.
  • Lifetime Premium:
    • Product ID: zudoku_lifetime_premium
    • Type: Non-Consumable (One-Time Purchase)
    • Features & Benefits: Permanently removes all mandatory ads and unlocks all themes forever with no recurring fees.
  • Restore Purchases: The Application supports both automatic and manual restoration of previously purchased products upon app launch and within the in-app store screen.
  • Upgrade Flow: Active monthly subscribers can seamlessly upgrade to Lifetime Premium at any time.
  • Financial Information: The Service Provider does not collect, store, or process any payment card details or banking credentials; all financial billing is handled strictly by Apple and Google.
